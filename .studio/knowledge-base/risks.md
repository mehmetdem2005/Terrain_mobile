# Risk Register

Risks identified during tickets. Maintained by Risk Officer.

## Format

```
## R-YYYY-NNN — <Title>
- Opened: YYYY-MM-DD (TKT-NNN)
- Likelihood: low | medium | high
- Impact: low | medium | high
- Description: [the risk]
- Mitigation: [plan to address]
- Tickets to address: [follow-up tickets if any]
- Status: OPEN | MITIGATED | ACCEPTED | CLOSED
```
